FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us your question and we'll reply on WhatsApp.

Which areas in Pune do you currently serve?

We are based in Wakad and actively serve clients across Pune and the greater Pune Metropolitan Region, including Hinjewadi, Baner, Aundh, and the central business districts. Reach out on WhatsApp to confirm coverage for your exact location.

Is your staff verified and background-checked?

Yes. Every housekeeping, security and manpower professional we deploy undergoes identity verification, address proof checks and police verification before being assigned to a site, and again periodically thereafter.

Can you handle both residential and commercial properties?

Absolutely. Our teams are structured to serve corporate offices, IT parks, residential societies, industrial facilities and retail spaces, each with a tailored scope and SOP.

How quickly can you start a new contract?

For most standard housekeeping and manpower deployments, we can mobilise a trained team within 3–7 working days of finalising the scope. Urgent requirements are assessed case by case.

Do you provide a single point of contact for multiple services?

Yes — that's the core of our Integrated Facility Management offering. One dedicated account manager coordinates housekeeping, security, maintenance and waste management so you don't have to.

How do I get a quote for my facility?

The fastest way is to message us directly on WhatsApp with your facility type, size and required services, or call us at +91 98508 19799. We'll schedule a site visit and share a customised proposal.
